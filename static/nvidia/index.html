Why NVIDIA

I've spent my career helping customers adopt whatever is next. Linux, then cloud, then Kubernetes, now AI at GPU scale.

Everything I help customers size and deploy runs on NVIDIA GPUs. The models, the inference, the training clusters. I want to go deeper into that layer instead of abstracting over it.

Moving to NVIDIA means influencing the product from the vendor side, working directly with the customers who push the limits I've been navigating from the outside.

NVIDIA is where all of this converges. You're not just building GPUs. You're building the entire stack from silicon to software. What cloud was in 2012, AI is today. I was there for cloud. I want to be here for AI.
